Growing Old

With You

March 19, 2026 plug

Remember when we forgot it was our monthiversary (apparently, this is the word we should’ve been using, Grammarly corrected me), and then unknowingly kind of still celebrated it together. It was when you made me try the chicken sandwich you love from SnR, the same day you gave me a paper bouquet, a love letter, and that mini book you made for V-Day. That incident was so funny to me ba cause I couldn’t believe we both forgot about it.

I remember days before that, we argued. Looking back, I still feel bad about reacting that way and making you overthink when I could’ve just communicated with you properly. I know that won’t be the last fight we’re gonna have. We’re still like, what, 10 months into our relationship, and for sure there's more challenges to come, and I hope to God we’ll be strong enough to get past them. There’ll be times when things might get too much or too hard, and our emotions will get ahead of us. When that happens, I hope in the end we’ll still choose each other and stay, not for the sake of staying but because we chose to stay, even during parts that make us want to choose otherwise. Please, I really wanna grow old with you.

Anyway, remembering that day when we forgot ba, I can’t help but giggle and daydream of the other things we’re going to experience together. I imagine the other things we’re gonna forget in the future and then laugh about. Could it be us forgetting there was food at home after getting back from eating out? Could it be us forgetting we had plans for the weekends, then ending up choosing to stay home and cuddle because it’s both our days off? Could it be our anniversary next cause we spend so much time together and we’re old? Although when we turn gray and frail, I'm gonna make sure there’s one thing we will never forget, which is that I have a bigger dick than you do.

I love you, my Chestnut!
